On withholding versus deleting. Withhold keeps a submission in your working copy but never publishes it. Delete destroys the image outright. If something arrives that must not exist anywhere — real personal details, anything unlawful — use Delete, and it is gone the moment you export again.

Pick the size first, then crop to fit it. Put the four handles on the real corners of the paper — they do not have to make a rectangle. A sheet photographed from an angle is a wonky quadrilateral, and Straighten to this maps it back onto a true rectangle, so what lands on the board is the square sheet it was before the camera got hold of it. The Desk places the handles for you, so most notices need nothing but the button. Fit that size pads the result out to the chosen size's proportions with the sheet's own paper colour, so it fills its place on the board exactly. Nothing inside your selection is ever cropped away or stretched — if a lot of the slip comes out blank, that is a sign the size is wrong for this sheet, and the Desk will say so. Edges brushes off a thumb or a shadow that survived; Tone settles the paper's colour against the board.
